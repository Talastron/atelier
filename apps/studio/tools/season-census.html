Season census

How many of your garments Today's Pick can actually suggest on a given day. An item's own season declaration is now a veto — a piece tagged Autumn/Winter is never suggested at 24°C — which is correct, and has a failure mode worth measuring rather than assuming: a British wardrobe skews cold, so if almost nothing qualifies on a warm day the card shows its empty state most days and reads as broken rather than careful.
